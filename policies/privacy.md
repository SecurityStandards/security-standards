# Privacy and Consent

`Revision 1.0`

[Company] is committed to protecting the privacy of our customers.

## Policy Statements

[Company] policy requires that:

(a) Privacy policy shall be made available to inform Customers how [Company]
collects, uses, secures and shares customer information. 

(b) Valid consent must be obtained for data collected from a Customer and the
purposes data is used for must be provided.  Customer must be provided an option
to opt-in or opt-out.
